## Questions

H2: Contract and wallet questions

1. Is this traditional crop insurance? No. TERROWA is an experimental smart-contract coverage pool and has not been presented as a licensed insurance product.
2. What does the wallet approve? Connection, a BOT value transfer for cover or stake, and the contract call shown in the wallet prompt.
3. Where is evidence stored? The contract stores the CID. The evidence file remains on IPFS or the storage service that pins it.
4. Who pays a claim? The appointed assessor approves or rejects it. Approval transfers BOT from available contract liquidity.
5. Can I always withdraw a stake? No. The seven-day lock must pass, and the pool must remain solvent after the withdrawal.
