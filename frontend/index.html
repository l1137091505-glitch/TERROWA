Claims carry evidence

  1. File the loss record

    The farmer sends a policy ID, requested amount, short loss record and one IPFS CID.

  2. Review the claim

    The appointed assessor checks the record and writes an approval or rejection note on-chain.

  3. Pay from the reserve

    An approved amount transfers only when the remaining balance can still support every active policy.

Contract and wallet questions

The limits matter before a wallet signs.

Open BOT Chain explorer

Is this traditional crop insurance?

No. TERROWA is an experimental smart-contract coverage pool and has not been presented as a licensed insurance product.

What does the wallet approve?

Connection, a BOT value transfer for cover or stake, and the contract call shown in the wallet prompt.

Where is evidence stored?

The contract stores the CID. The evidence file remains on IPFS or the storage service that pins it.

Who pays a claim?

The appointed assessor approves or rejects it. Approval transfers BOT from available contract liquidity.

Can I always withdraw a stake?

No. The seven-day lock must pass, and the pool must remain solvent after the withdrawal.
